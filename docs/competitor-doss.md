# Competitor notes: DOSS (doss.com)

Reviewed 2026-09-12 (homepage and main navigation). Use this for positioning and content planning. Do not copy their wording or claims; write our own, based on what we can prove.

## How they position themselves

- An AI-native ERP / operations platform aimed mainly at consumer goods brands (also food and beverage, health and beauty, manufacturing, distribution).
- Core promise: one reliable source of truth across inventory, costs, orders and procurement, across every sales channel.
- Sales motion: a "Book a demo" button after almost every section, plus a sales contact form at the bottom.
- Look: near-black neutral background, white type, product screenshots. This is likely why our navy dark theme felt "blue" by comparison.

## Homepage structure

1. Hero: category claim, one-line outcome, demo button.
2. "Operations as a growth engine": tabs for orders, inventory, procurement, new channels, contribution margin and accounting integration, each with short outcome bullets.
3. Client logos.
4. Four case-study testimonials, each with a named person, their role and two hard metrics (hours saved, faster invoicing, fewer errors).
5. Outcome statement with a CTA.
6. Grid of 8 modules, ending with an offer to build anything missing.
7. "Built for your industry" (4 industries).
8. Closing CTA and a sales contact form.

Navigation: Products, Solutions (by module / by industry), Resources (news, case studies, guides, free tools such as an ERP readiness assessment and a purchase order generator), Integrations, Careers, Sign in.

## What changed on our site after this review

- **Modules section:** added a "Need a module that isn't listed?" block with a consultation button (our own wording). A missing module is a common buyer worry; our fully custom promise answers it directly.
- **Dark theme:** moved from navy to neutral graphite with subtle light effects (see `docs/design-system.md`).
- No approved copy was replaced. Our content already covers their main topics; the gaps below need real information, not rewritten text.

## Recommended next (needs real information; never invent it)

1. **Proof section** after "Why businesses choose us": 3–6 client logos and 2–4 testimonials with name, role, company and one measurable result (e.g. time saved on quotations, faster month-end).
2. **Integrations strip** under Modules: only the systems we genuinely connect to (for example accounting software, GST e-invoicing, WhatsApp, e-commerce stores). Confirm the list with the team first.
3. **Industry pages** beyond manufacturing (distribution, retail, service) when content exists.
4. **A free tool** later, such as an "Is your business ready for a custom ERP?" checklist that ends in a consultation booking.
5. **Case studies**: one page per client story, linked from the proof section.
