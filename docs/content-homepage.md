## 3. Why businesses choose us

**Heading:** Built Around Your Business, Not the Other Way Around

- **Fast Delivery:** Most ERP rollouts run for six months or more. We scope, build, and deliver a working system in a few weeks — so you're running on it, not waiting on it.

## 4. How we work (process)

**Heading:** From Consultation to Live System in a Few Weeks

1. **Discovery Call:** We learn your workflow, current bottlenecks, and what a good system looks like for your business.
2. **Design & Planning:** We map out the modules, data flow, and AI agents your system needs.
3. **Build & AI Integration:** We build the full system and wire in the AI agents for the tasks you want automated.
4. **Deploy & Launch:** Your system goes live, your team gets a walkthrough, and we stay on for support.

## 5. AI agents in your ERP

**Heading:** Not Just Software — A System That Works For You

Every ERP we build can include AI agents that actively do work inside your system, not just store data:

- **AI Sales Assistant:** drafts quotations and responds to RFQs automatically based on your pricing rules.
- **AI Inventory Forecaster:** flags low stock and predicts reorder points before you run out.
- **AI Customer Support Agent:** answers common customer questions about orders and stock status.
- **AI Data Entry Agent:** reads incoming purchase orders, invoices, or vendor quotes and enters them into the system automatically.
- **AI Report Generator:** turns your raw data into plain-language summaries for sales, stock, and purchasing on demand.

## 6. What we build: core ERP modules

**Heading:** Every Module Your Business Needs, Custom-Fit

- **Customer & Vendor Management:** A complete database of customers and vendors for efficient communication and transaction tracking.
- **Product Catalog & Pricing:** A detailed, centralized list of products and pricing kept consistent and accurate across the system.
- **Automated Price Update Chain:** Product prices update automatically when raw material costs change, keeping semi-finished and finished goods pricing in sync.
- **Request for Quotation (RFQ):** Send RFQs to vendors and compare quotes side by side to choose the best supplier.
- **Sales Orders:** Convert quotations into orders and track them from confirmation through fulfillment.
- **Quotations:** Generate professional, accurate quotations that communicate pricing and product details clearly to customers.
- **Indent Management:** Raise and track internal material requests so procurement always has visibility into what's needed and when.
- **Purchase Orders:** Create and track purchase orders for raw materials and inventory, with full control over procurement.
- **Inventory Management:** Track raw materials, semi-finished, and finished goods to avoid stockouts and excess inventory.
- **Composite & Bundled Products:** Handle multi-part or bundled products with accurate inventory and pricing across the whole structure.
- **Stock Adjustment:** Correct inventory levels for discrepancies or damage while keeping records accurate.
- **Real-Time Stock Updates:** Live visibility into stock movement from purchases, sales, manufacturing, and adjustments.
- **Vendor Portal:** A dedicated space for vendors to manage orders, view purchase history, and update product info.
- **Customer Portal:** Customers can check order status, track shipments, and browse product info securely online.

## 9. FAQ

- **How can you deliver a full ERP in a few weeks?** We keep scope tight and focused on what your business actually needs, use a proven module library as the foundation, and customize on top of it — instead of building everything from zero.
- **Is it really custom, or a template with my logo on it?** Every system is built around your specific workflow, data, and pricing rules. The AI agents and modules are configured for your business, not reused as-is.
- **Do you support the system after launch?** Yes — you work directly with the team that built it for fixes, changes, and new features as your business grows.
- **What industries do you build for?** Manufacturing is a strong fit today, and the same core system adapts to distribution, retail, and service-based businesses with their own workflows.
- **What does "AI agents in the ERP" actually mean?** It means parts of the system act on your behalf — drafting quotations, flagging low stock, answering routine customer questions — instead of just storing data for you to act on manually.
- **How much does a custom ERP cost?** Every system is scoped to what your business actually needs, so pricing varies. After our discovery call we provide a clear, fixed quote — no surprise invoices or hidden fees.
- **Can you migrate data from our current system?** Yes. We handle data migration from spreadsheets, legacy software, or other ERPs as part of the build, so your team starts with everything already in place.
- **How do you handle security and data privacy?** Your system runs on infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access controls, and regular backups. We scope security to your industry's requirements during the discovery phase.
